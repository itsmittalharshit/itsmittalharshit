I build machine learning systems that make it out of the notebook — into a container, onto a device, in front of the person who actually needs the answer. Most of my work sits where model quality meets delivery: imbalanced data, tight latency budgets, and environments with no reliable internet.

---

### What I bring to a team

**Models that survive contact with real data.** Class imbalance, noisy labels and long-tail cases are where accuracy quietly dies. My research work cut information loss to **7%** and lifted accuracy by **up to 10%** across peer-reviewed frameworks by fixing the activation and class-balancing layer rather than throwing more parameters at the problem.

**Delivery, not just experiments.** Containerised ML pipelines with Docker and Git so results reproduce across a team, and a version-controlled path from a training run to something deployable.

**Edge and offline first.** Full products that run with zero backend: on-device face recognition at 128-d embeddings, local SQLite persistence, geo-fencing — shipped as working apps, not prototypes.

**Deployed under real constraints.** At DRDO I built a 3D facial recognition pipeline that turned CCTV footage into point clouds and went into an active security setup.
