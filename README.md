## Harshit Mittal

**AI engineer | data science** 

I build machine learning systems and care most about the part where they meet real users: a clinician looking at a scan, a shopkeeper closing the day's books, a team that needs a model to run on a phone with no signal.

Currently finishing an MSc in Advanced Computer Science at the University of Leeds. My thesis, **SAFViT**, is a hierarchical encoder–decoder architecture for cell segmentation that improves detection of minority dead-cell classes on the PanNuke dataset. Before Leeds I worked on medical AI research at USIC&T and a 3D facial recognition pipeline at CFEES, DRDO.

Open to AI engineering and data science roles in the UK and India.
